# Statutory Customs & Shipping Regulatory Compliance

## Authoritative Regulatory Framework

FraudShield evaluates pre-shipment consignments against verified statutory regulations in India and international cross-border corridors:

### 1. Customs Act, 1962 (Act No. 52 of 1962)
- **Section 50**: Entry of goods for exportation. Mandatory declaration of accurate commercial description, weight, and true transaction value.
- **Section 46**: Entry of goods on importation (Bill of Entry).
- **Section 111 & 113**: Confiscation of improperly imported/exported goods for false or misdeclared specifications.
- **Source**: Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
- **URL**: `https://www.cbic.gov.in/htdocs-cbec/customs/cs-act/cs-act-idx`

### 2. Courier Imports and Exports (Clearance) Regulations, 1998
- **Regulation 5(1)**: Commercial consignment threshold for simplified courier clearance (Packages exceeding ₹50,000 or 70 kg require formal commercial Shipping Bill/Bill of Entry filing and verified Importer-Exporter Code).
- **Regulation 12**: Mandatory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) documents and identity authentication for all commercial consignors.
- **Source**: CBIC Circular 30/2020-Customs.

### 3. Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 (Act No. 44 of 1958)
- **Section 334 & 335**: Carriage of dangerous goods. Mandatory verified container weight certificate and Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) prior to carrier loading for heavy or hazardous cargo (>100 kg).
- **Source**: Directorate General of Shipping, Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India.

### 4. Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 & PMLA Rules
- **Section 7 & PMLA Rule 9(1)**: Verification of beneficial corporate ownership for cross-border transit logistics and export data processing (EDPMS) reconciliation.
- **Source**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction on Export of Goods and Services.

## Critical Legal Distinction

FraudShield explicitly enforces and displays on all UI screens and generated PDF reports:
> **"This is an automated compliance verification record and is not a government-issued approval."**

The platform evaluates procedural conformity and alerts carrier compliance officers to statutory discrepancies before cargo is accepted for transit.
